Evidence Verification Portal

Prepare a setup plan

This page only prepares a plan

It checks what you enter and shows exactly what applying the plan would write. Then it gives you the plan as a file. It writes nothing, keeps nothing and enrols nothing, and the portal forgets the plan as soon as it has answered.

You apply the plan yourself, outside the browser: with Administrator setup in the application's menu, or with admin-apply in Terminal. When you apply it, you type each issuer's fingerprint again, from what the issuer gave you by a call or another route. Do not copy it from this page. The plan is applied only if every fingerprint matches the key in the plan.

Never paste a private key here. Only the issuer's public key belongs in a plan. The portal refuses anything that looks like a private key and does not show it back.

Verification unavailable

The verification engine could not be loaded or its installation could not be validated. No plan can be prepared.
